Road Lab — lanes are data, everything else is derived.
V select · R draw road · F fit · G snap · T traffic · L lane graph
Draw: click points · click a node/road to connect · double-click / Enter to end · right-click ends · Backspace undoes a point · Alt = no snap
Select: drag nodes and bend points (drop a node on another to merge) · double-click a road adds a bend · Del deletes
Pan: drag empty space, right/middle drag, or Space+drag · wheel zooms · Ctrl+Z / Ctrl+Y
