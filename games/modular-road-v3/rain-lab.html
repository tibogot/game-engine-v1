A drop is millimetres from the lens and hopelessly out of focus, so what it gathers arrives as a soft milky smear — not a crisp inverted photograph. Switch it off to see the decal version. The taps sit behind the coverage test, so they run on the ~10% of pixels a drop is actually on; it is a build-time switch, so off means absent, not zeroed.

Parameters

amount is the master. bead* are the drops that cling, runner* the ones that let go and leave a stippled trail. invert and magnify are the lens: a drop forms a real, flipped image of a region magnify wide, so raising it is what makes drops look like glass rather than plastic. Drop invert to 0 for the ordinary smear every other implementation does, and compare. lean is airflow, driven by road speed in game.
