The test is the orbit. A cone card looks fine from one angle and like a party hat from the next, so press O and watch the beam go all the way round: brightest looking into it, clearly there broadside, faintest from behind — and never switching off.

Four layers stack here, and they are separable on purpose. Turn the beam off and the pool + glare alone already read as "lights on". Turn lit rain off and the beam loses most of its body — the particles inside it are doing more work than the haze.

Measured on this machine: both beams together cost ~0.67 ms at 20 steps, ~1.0 ms at 32 — and 16 looks the same as 32, so the step slider is the first thing to spend or save on.
