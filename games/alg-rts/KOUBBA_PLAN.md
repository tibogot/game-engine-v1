## What a rural Maghreb koubba is (references)

The tomb of a local saint (wali) — what the French called a "marabout": ONE square room under a white dome, on the
edge of a village or its cemetery, on a crest or a pass. Valued for what it means, not for ornament: plain lime
render, rebuilt and re-whitewashed by the village for generations. Sources: [Qubba — Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Qubba),
P. Raffa, *The marabout* ([Disegnarecon](https://disegnarecon.univaq.it/ojs/index.php/disegnarecon/article/view/775)),
[Sidi Mojluf marabout — Archnet](https://next.archnet.org/sites/6354), rural Moroccan / Algerian marabout photos.

## The checklist (what the model has)

- [x] **Soft, thick, hand-made**: rubble walls under layers of lime — rounded edges and corners (a 14 cm bevel, not a
      box), the walls leaning in a little (a batter), nothing ruled.
- [x] **A worn whitewash**, not white plastic: off-white and cream, grey rain streaks down from the parapet, a dirty
      ochre band at the foot (splash, dust), the render FALLEN in patches low on the walls — the rubble stone under it.
- [x] **A stone footing** proud of the walls, the ground's colour.
- [x] **The roof's PARAPET** with rounded top and the four corner HORNS (stepped pointed merlons — the Algerian koubba's
      mark), a drip of render under each.
- [x] **The DOME** smooth (turned, 40 sides — no facets), slightly POINTED (ovoid, not a half ball), on a low
      OCTAGONAL DRUM with a lip; the finial: an iron rod, three copper balls and a crescent.
- [x] **An ARCHED door** (round-headed) deep in the thick wall, its lime frame proud of the wall, a low green plank door,
      a worn threshold stone.
- [x] **Two candle NICHES** either side of the door, soot-blackened, a clay lamp in each.
- [x] **Small high windows** with a wooden grille.
- [x] **A bench** (dukkana) along the front for visitors.
- [x] **Offerings and rags**: jars and a pot by the door, coloured rags tied on a cord at the door (votive).
- [x] **Its enclosure (hawsh)**: a low dry-stone wall on an IRREGULAR outline (not a square), its top broken, a gateway
      between two whitewashed pillars with little domed caps; one family grave inside.
- [x] **States**: damaged — the dome holed (dark inside), a horn knocked off, the render blown off one wall, a wall of
      the enclosure down; ruin — the dome fallen in, the walls' stumps, a heap of white rubble and stone.
