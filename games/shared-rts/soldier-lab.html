Built by node tools/packMixamo.mjs --skip soldier3 --rig-texture originalsoldier from assets-src/soldiers/: the FBX rig + clips, and every GLB soldier given the rig's skeleton. Clip names are the FBX file names. Weapon: hung on the pack's weapon bone. Firing / aiming clips are SHOULDERED (butt in the shoulder pocket, forestock in the left palm, the right arm bent onto the grip); carry clips hold it hand to hand. Judge it on the RIFLE clips (the unarmed ones swing it). The grip editor sets the hand-placed corrections (soldierGrips.js). Skin weights: a patch of the wrong colour (a sleeve coloured like the torso, a pouch like the thigh) is where a transferred soldier will stretch. The treadmill speed is measured from the clip (the planted foot slides back at exactly the ground speed), so it is the unit speed that walks without sliding.
